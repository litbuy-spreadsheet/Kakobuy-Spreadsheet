Your pre-order checklist

  1. Save the source. Record the seller URL, listing identifier if available, variant and date checked. Similar photos do not prove two listings offer the same item.
  2. Confirm the offer. Check color, size, quantity, included parts and availability. Ask about unclear descriptions.
  3. Compare measurements. Use a similar item you own, measured the same way as the seller's chart.
  4. Review evidence. Find photos relevant to the exact variant and note their source and stated date. Request missing views.
  5. Check the destination. Select Kakobuy, open the purchase link and verify the product again on the agent's site.
  6. Review costs and terms. Confirm fees, delivery assumptions and the applicable return process before ordering.

Use measurements, not just labels

M and XL are labels, not measurements. Choose a comparable item that fits, lay it out and use the seller's measuring method. Keep units consistent and distinguish flat widths from circumferences.

Fit checks by item type
ItemCompareClarify
Tops & hoodiesChest width, length, shoulder, sleeveFlat width or circumference; measuring points
PantsWaist, rise, thigh, inseamRelaxed or stretched waist; intended cut
ShoesRelevant measurements against a pair that fitsMethod and whether it reflects usable space
AccessoriesDimensions, strap length, included partsUnits and exact variant

Ask for measurements when a chart is incomplete. A photo of a size tag cannot resolve a measurement mismatch.

Read QC photos with a question in mind

Warehouse quality-control photos can help inspect the received item. Start with what you need to establish.

  • Identity: Do color, size and item count match your order?
  • Appearance: Are there visible marks, damage, uneven panels or mismatched left/right parts?
  • Construction: Can you see relevant seams, closures and hardware?
  • Fit: Do requested measurement photos answer your question?
  • Missing evidence: Would another angle or close-up help before you decide?
Lighting affects apparent color. Photos alone cannot establish authenticity, long-term durability or material composition. Available evidence varies by listing.

Estimate the full order cost

Use one currency and fill in the costs that apply to your order. Keep unknown amounts marked as unknown.

Item price × quantity
+ seller-to-warehouse delivery
+ applicable agent or service fees
+ international shipping estimate
+ applicable taxes, duties and payment/currency fees
= estimated total

Confirm parcel dimensions, billable weight, route and current terms with your agent. An estimate is not a quote, and a catalog price is not the delivered total.

Example: researching a hoodie

This illustrates a workflow, not a tested product recommendation.

  1. Save the listing URL, selected color and seller size chart.
  2. Measure chest width and length on a hoodie you like wearing.
  3. Compare like-for-like measurements. Ask about unclear methods instead of assuming a size conversion.
  4. After warehouse arrival, check the received variant, visible construction and requested measurements.
  5. Confirm shipping estimates and your available options before deciding whether to ship.
Example research note: “Variant checked; chest measurement method unclear; requested flat chest and body-length photos; shipping estimate pending.”

Common questions

Is this an official Kakobuy guide?

No. This independent resource links to Fashionreps. Platform and brand names identify relevant services or listings without implying endorsement.

Where do I pay for an order?

On the selected shopping agent's website. Confirm the order details, payment terms and shipping with that agent.

What if a product link is outdated?

Check whether it opens the expected item. Report the URL, actual result and date checked through the GitHub issue link below. Keep private account and order information out of public issues.

How often are listings updated?

This guide does not promise a refresh interval for external listings. Check the current product page. Repository history records guide edits, not the freshness of every listing.

Does every product have QC photos?

Check each listing for available evidence. Photos are not guaranteed for every item, and a photo set cannot answer every quality question.
