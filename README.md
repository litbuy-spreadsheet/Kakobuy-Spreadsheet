A practical directory and research checklist for shoppers exploring products before ordering through Kakobuy.

## Start here

1. Open the catalog and choose a category.
2. Save the exact seller URL, variant and date checked.
3. Compare the size chart with an item you already own.
4. Review available buyer notes and QC evidence.
5. Choose Kakobuy in the Fashionreps agent selector before opening a purchase link.
6. Confirm the destination product, current price, fees and shipping terms with the agent.

Orders, payments and shipping are handled on the selected agent's website.

## Research checklist

| Check | Record | Pause when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listing | Seller URL, identifier, variant, date checked | The link opens a different product |
| Fit | Measurements, units and measuring method | Only a size label is available |
| Evidence | Relevant images, their source and stated date | Photos show another variant or leave questions unanswered |
| Budget | Item cost, delivery, fees and shipping assumptions | An important cost remains unknown |
| Order | Selected agent, color, size and quantity | The destination does not match your choice |

### Size and fit

Compare a similar item that fits you with the seller's chart, using the same measurement method. For tops, record chest width, length, shoulder and sleeve. Distinguish flat width from circumference. For pants, compare waist, rise, thigh and inseam. For shoes, ask for relevant measurements and compare with a pair that fits; size labels alone are insufficient. Clarify missing details rather than assuming a conversion.

### QC review

Warehouse quality-control photos can help inspect the received item. Check variant, quantity, visible marks, seams, closures and matching left/right parts. Request additional angles or measurements when needed. Lighting affects apparent color. Photos alone do not establish authenticity, durability or material composition; evidence coverage varies by listing.

### Budget worksheet

```text
Item price × quantity
+ seller-to-warehouse delivery
+ applicable agent/service fees
+ international shipping estimate
+ applicable taxes, duties and payment/currency fees
= estimated total
```

Confirm currency, parcel dimensions, billable weight, route and current terms with your agent. Keep unknown amounts marked as unknown. A catalog price is not the delivered total, and an estimate is not a quote.

## Example research note

For a hoodie, save the URL and chosen color, compare its chest width and body length with your own hoodie, and ask for measurements if the chart is unclear. After warehouse arrival, check the received variant and requested photos before deciding whether to ship.

Example note: “Variant checked; chest measurement method unclear; requested flat chest and body-length photos; shipping estimate pending.” This illustrates a workflow, not a tested product recommendation.

## FAQ

**Is this a downloadable spreadsheet?** This is a guide to a web-based product catalog, not an Excel or Google Sheets download.

**Is this official Kakobuy documentation?** No. This repository links to Fashionreps and is independent of Kakobuy. Platform and brand names identify services or listings without implying endorsement.

**Does every product have QC photos?** Check each listing. This guide does not guarantee QC coverage for every item.

**Where do I order?** On the agent's site after confirming product details and current terms.
